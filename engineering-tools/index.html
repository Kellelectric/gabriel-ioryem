These tools are intentionally preliminary. They explain basic relationships; they do not select cable sizes, protective devices, inverter models or final system configurations.

Load current estimate

Estimate steady-state current from electrical input power.

01
Estimated line current24.15
A

Formula: 1φ: I = P ÷ (V × PF × η). 3φ: I = P ÷ (√3 × V × PF × η). Power is treated as real input power.

Excludes motor starting current, harmonics, demand/diversity, unbalance and transient loading. Confirm whether the entered power is input or output power before using the result.

Resistive voltage drop

A simplified conductor-resistance estimate.

02
Estimated voltage drop5.51
V · 2.40%

Approximation: 1φ / DC: ΔV = 2 × I × L × ρ ÷ S. Balanced 3φ: ΔV = √3 × I × L × ρ ÷ S. Resistivity values are nominal at 20°C.

This is a resistance-only estimate. It excludes conductor operating-temperature correction, AC reactance, power factor, harmonics, joints and installation conditions. It is not a cable-sizing approval.

Solar PV array estimate

Estimate array capacity from daily energy and solar resource.

03
Indicative PV array rating4.00
kWp

Formula: PV array (kWp) ≈ daily energy (kWh) ÷ [peak sun hours × performance ratio].

A first-pass energy estimate only. Actual yield depends on location and season, shading, orientation, temperature, soiling, system losses, clipping and load timing. Use site-specific solar data for design.

Battery energy capacity

Estimate nominal storage for a defined critical-load autonomy.

04
Indicative nominal capacity11.11
kWh

Formula: nominal capacity ≈ daily critical-load energy × autonomy ÷ (usable DoD × discharge-path efficiency).

Does not check battery power rating, surge capability, temperature derating, ageing reserve, BMS limits, charge rate or inverter compatibility. Confirm manufacturer limits and required end-of-life autonomy.

Engineering responsibility and limitations

All outputs are indicative calculations based only on the values entered. They are not a quotation, electrical design, installation instruction, compliance certificate or substitute for inspection and verification. Final engineering must consider applicable Nigerian requirements, relevant IEC/BS standards, equipment manufacturer data, site conditions, protection coordination, earthing, environmental factors and competent professional review. No values entered here are transmitted or stored by this page.
