rationale: Engineered, not decorated. Quiet authority is replaced by a working, tactile identity. Copper is the material of the trade, so it replaces the gold that makes many professional sites look alike. Signal yellow is used only for things that are live, such as lamps and the main breaker. Technical labels use a monospace face, like annotations on a drawing.
notes: Copper-ink is the accessible copper for small text on light surfaces (at least 5.5:1). Bright copper is for dark surfaces and non-text lines (at least 5.4:1 on forest). Signal yellow is reserved for live indicators such as the main breaker and switched-on toggles. Sage is a cool light surface that breaks up the dark sections. Focus rings are two-tone (forest inside copper) so they stay visible on dark and light surfaces. Discipline icons live in assets/icons.svg (24px grid, 1.5 stroke, currentColor). The header is dark glass with a pill navigation and a separate copper call to action. The hero is a dark forest drawing cover sheet with service chips, floating copper chips and sparks, followed by an interactive distribution-board dashboard. Its main breaker and toggle switches drive the building drawing, an illustrative daily load chart and the project brief. Project references are a schedule table, and the footer is a title block.
