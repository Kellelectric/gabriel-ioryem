# Gabriel Ioryem — Personal Executive Design System

## Overview

This design system establishes the visual identity for the personal executive website of Gabriel Ioryem, Founder & CEO of Kell Electricals Ltd. The creative direction sits at the intersection of international executive editorial design, architectural and engineering publications, contemporary luxury personal branding, and refined independent creative portfolios.

The website is conceived as an established engineering entrepreneur’s personal digital journal combined with a sophisticated executive portfolio. It should feel like the opening pages of a premium engineering monograph or a high-end founder profile published by a serious business publication — confident, precise, restrained, and technically intelligent.

Visual philosophy: **Quiet authority through material honesty and typographic discipline.**  
The design avoids the visual language of SaaS marketing sites, generic CEO templates, and corporate brochures. Instead it draws from the material qualities of architectural drawings, engineering documentation, and carefully printed executive publications: generous margins, deliberate hierarchy, warm paper-like surfaces, and a single deep green that carries both the weight of infrastructure and the calm of long-term thinking.

Colour is used sparingly and purposefully. Typography carries the primary expressive load. Photography (when present) is treated editorially — never decorative filler. Motion is restrained and purposeful. Negative space is treated as an active design element rather than empty region.

The result must be unmistakably personal to Gabriel while maintaining a quiet, respectful connection to the Kell Electricals identity (deep forest green) without becoming a company brochure.

## Colors

The palette is built around a deep forest green drawn from Kell Electricals’ existing identity, elevated into a more editorial and executive register. It is supported by warm ivory surfaces, graphite text, and a single sophisticated gold accent used with extreme restraint.

**Rationale**  
- Forest green anchors the identity in engineering and infrastructure while remaining sophisticated rather than corporate.  
- Warm ivory replaces pure white to give the site a printed, material quality rather than a digital-screen coldness.  
- Gold is deliberately muted and used sparingly so that its appearance feels intentional and valuable.  
- Graphite and slate provide clear hierarchy without relying on colour for differentiation.  
- The overall system stays within a restrained family of warm neutrals + one deep chromatic colour.

## Typography

Typography is the primary vehicle of character. The system pairs a distinctive editorial serif for display and major headings with a highly legible, refined sans-serif for interface and body text.

Cormorant Garamond provides the editorial, almost classical gravitas expected of a serious founder’s presence. Inter supplies the modern, precise, highly readable counterpart required for body copy, navigation, and forms.
