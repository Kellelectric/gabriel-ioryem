PROJECT 01 / RESIDENTIAL DEVELOPMENT

A residential development project within the Life Camp area. The project forms part of the built-environment experience associated with Kell Electricals Ltd.

Engineering perspective

  • Understanding the electrical requirements of a multi-unit residential environment.

PROJECT 02 / APARTMENT DEVELOPMENT

Drol Apartments

Jahi · Abuja, Nigeria

An apartment development in Jahi, representing the residential building-services context in which electrical systems must serve both individual occupiers and shared building functions.

Engineering perspective

  • Considering distribution requirements across residential spaces and common areas.
  • Planning for safe access, protection, practical maintenance and future service needs.
  • Aligning electrical installation work with the wider construction programme.
Specific system architecture, quantities, brands and commissioning results are intentionally not stated until confirmed against the project documentation.
PROJECT 03 / INSTITUTIONAL FACILITY

An institutional and multipurpose facility project in Garki II. This setting brings a different operational context from residential developments, with the building’s intended use informing engineering coordination.

Engineering perspective

  • Interpreting facility requirements and the operational needs of its users.
  • Considering distribution, protection and coordination within a multi-purpose building.
  • Prioritising safe, understandable and maintainable engineering outcomes.
