01 / About

An engineer at heart. A builder by instinct.

My work is driven by a simple belief: engineering should make life safer, systems more dependable and businesses better equipped to serve people.

With a background in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, I work across technical problem-solving, project delivery and business development. My experience spans electrical installations, power systems, renewable energy, building services and integrated technologies.

I’m interested in the full picture: understanding the requirement, making sound technical decisions, coordinating execution and building systems that remain useful long after installation.
