06 / Engineering notes

The technical thinking behind reliable systems.

Good engineering is not simply the installation of equipment. It is the reasoning that determines what should be installed, how it should be protected and how it will perform.

Select a topic to explore the principles I consider when assessing and developing electrical systems.

ENGINEERING NOTE / 01⌁

Protection begins with understanding the fault.

Electrical protection is a coordinated system of devices and design decisions. Circuit breakers, fuses, residual-current protection and earthing arrangements must be selected for the installation, not simply chosen by rating.

Fault current and disconnection timeProtective-device coordinationEarthing and bonding integrity

Technical explanations are general engineering principles. Final selection and compliance checks must be based on the site, applicable standards and verified calculations.
