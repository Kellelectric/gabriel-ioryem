About Gabriel

Engineer.
Builder.
Leader.

Gabriel Ioryem is a COREN-registered electrical engineer and the Founder & CEO of Kell Electricals Ltd, an electrical engineering company based in Abuja, Nigeria.

His work sits at the intersection of technical depth and practical execution. Over more than fifteen years he has led the design, installation and delivery of electrical systems across residential estates, commercial buildings and industrial facilities — always with the same underlying standard: do the job once, do it right, and leave the client with something they can trust.

The entrepreneurial path began with a clear observation: reliable electrical work in Nigeria requires more than technical skill. It requires disciplined project management, transparent communication, proper documentation and a long-term view of quality. Kell Electricals was built around those principles.

Today the company has completed more than a thousand projects across twenty Abuja districts and beyond, spanning power systems, solar and hybrid solutions, industrial installations, smart systems and integrated security. Gabriel continues to lead both the technical direction and the business development of the organisation.

His professional philosophy is simple and demanding: engineering integrity, accountability in delivery, continuous improvement, and sustainable business practice. He believes that the systems we build should outlast the projects that create them.
