About Gabriel

Engineer.
Builder.
Leader.

Gabriel Ioryem is a COREN-registered electrical engineer and the Founder & CEO of Kell Electricals Ltd, an electrical engineering company based in Abuja, Nigeria.

His work sits at the intersection of technical depth and practical execution. Over more than fifteen years he has led the design, installation and delivery of electrical systems across residential estates, commercial buildings and industrial facilities.

Today the company has completed more than a thousand projects across twenty Abuja districts and beyond. Gabriel continues to lead both the technical direction and the business development of the organisation.
