GABRIEL IORYEM

SHORT PROFESSIONAL BIOGRAPHY

Gabriel Ioryem is an electrical engineer, entrepreneur and the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Kell Electricals Ltd, an Abuja-based electrical engineering and technical services company established in 2010.

He holds a Bachelor of Engineering (B.Eng.) in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Madonna University, Nigeria, and brings more than 15 years of professional experience to the electrical engineering and technical services field.

His professional practice spans electrical installations and power distribution, solar energy and battery storage, generator integration, building services, smart automation, CCTV, access control and related power-continuity systems. His approach combines engineering judgement with practical problem-solving, careful coordination and a focus on safe, maintainable outcomes.

As the leader of Kell Electricals Ltd, he provides direction across the business and its engineering service delivery, with an emphasis on safety, accuracy, workmanship, maintainability and accountability.
