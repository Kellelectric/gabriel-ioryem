Renewable energy

Hybrid Solar Power & Battery Storage

A hybrid system should be sized around the energy the building needs, the hours it needs it and the level of backup expected.

Original project image to be addedReserved for approved site photographs, drawings or installation details

The challenge

A property with an unreliable grid supply may have a mixture of essential and discretionary loads. Selecting panels and an inverter from a single headline wattage can overlook daily energy consumption, motor starting current, battery autonomy and periods of low solar production.

Engineering approach

The work is approached in stages, with design decisions tied to the actual site conditions, equipment data and applicable requirements.

  1. Collect appliance ratings and operating-hour information to build a realistic load profile.
  2. Separate essential circuits from loads that can be shed during an outage.
  3. Estimate daily energy demand and define the required backup duration.
  4. Size the inverter, PV array and battery bank as a coordinated system, checking surge capability and usable battery capacity.
  5. Specify appropriate DC and AC isolation, overcurrent protection, surge protection, earthing and cable routes.
  6. Commission the system and verify operating modes, charging behaviour, transfer functions and monitoring.

Intended outcome

The intended result is a balanced hybrid power arrangement that prioritises agreed essential loads, makes better use of available solar energy and reduces reliance on generator operation when conditions permit.

Key engineering considerations

Load profile, inverter continuous and surge ratings, battery usable capacity, PV yield, shading, protection coordination, ventilation and manufacturer requirements.
