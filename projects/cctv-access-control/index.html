LAYER 01 / 05

Camera coverage

Camera placement begins with the scene: what must be seen, at what distance, under what lighting and with what level of detail. A camera count alone is not a coverage plan.

Design check: map fields of view, mounting heights, lighting and privacy boundaries.

Conceptual architecture only. Final design must account for site survey, privacy obligations, network security, storage calculations, equipment compatibility and local operating requirements.

The challenge

A collection of cameras does not automatically create effective surveillance. Blind spots, poor lighting, unsuitable lens selection, limited network capacity and inadequate recording retention can undermine the system. Access control also needs a clear permissions structure.

Engineering approach

The work is approached in stages, with design decisions tied to the actual site conditions, equipment data and applicable requirements.

  1. Walk the site and map entrances, circulation routes and priority observation areas.
  2. Select camera types, lens views and mounting positions for each scene.
  3. Plan structured cabling, network capacity, power and equipment locations.
  4. Configure recording, storage and retention settings against agreed requirements.
  5. Integrate access-control readers and door hardware where specified, with role-based permissions.
  6. Test live views, playback, time synchronisation, alerts and user access; provide handover guidance.

Intended outcome

The intended result is a coordinated surveillance and access-management arrangement with defined coverage, centralised viewing and a more consistent process for managing permissions.

Key engineering considerations

Field of view, lighting, image quality, retention period, network security, privacy, power resilience and controlled access to recordings.

Engineering design note

Design basis & verification

The following are typical inputs and checks for this class of system. They are a planning aid, not evidence that a specific site has been surveyed, designed or commissioned.

Design inputs to confirm

  • Security objective, areas to observe, camera fields of view and required image detail
  • Day/night lighting, mounting constraints, privacy boundaries and retention policy
  • Network bandwidth, PoE budget, storage capacity and power-backup requirements
  • User roles, remote access, network segmentation and recording access controls

Verification before handover

  • Camera framing, focus and coverage in representative lighting conditions
  • Time synchronisation, recording, playback, export and retention behaviour
  • Role-based access, credential handling and network isolation
  • Power resilience, outage recovery and privacy masking where required

Final values, protective-device selections, test methods and acceptance criteria must be established from verified site conditions, equipment data and applicable Nigerian requirements and relevant installation standards.
