The challenge

A building using utility supply and a standby generator needs a controlled method of transferring between sources. The arrangement must prevent unsafe interconnection, account for generator capability and behave predictably when either source becomes unavailable.

Engineering approach

The work is approached in stages, with design decisions tied to the actual site conditions, equipment data and applicable requirements.

  1. Survey the existing supply, generator data and essential-load requirements.
  2. Confirm generator capacity against steady-state demand, starting loads and operating conditions.
  3. Select a suitably rated transfer arrangement and verify source and fault ratings.
  4. Design control wiring, interlocking, sensing and transfer sequence to suit the equipment.
  5. Coordinate earthing, neutral switching and protective arrangements with the system design.
  6. Test simulated source failure, generator start, transfer, retransfer and abnormal conditions.

Intended outcome

The intended result is a coordinated backup-power arrangement that reduces manual changeover activity and supports controlled transfer between the available sources.

Key engineering considerations

Generator derating, transient response, ATS ratings, neutral and earthing configuration, interlocking, load priority, ventilation and exhaust safety.

Engineering design note

Design basis & verification

The following are typical inputs and checks for this class of system. They are a planning aid, not evidence that a specific site has been surveyed, designed or commissioned.

Design inputs to confirm

  • Measured demand, load steps, power factor and motor-starting requirements
  • Generator rating after applicable environmental derating and transient response
  • ATS current rating, pole arrangement, source sensing and interlocking method
  • Neutral/earthing arrangement, fuel, exhaust, ventilation and safe access

Verification before handover

  • Phase sequence and correct source sensing on both supplies
  • Transfer/retransfer timing and electrical/mechanical interlock operation
  • Emergency stop, alarms, protection and loss-of-source response
  • Functional tests under representative load and verification of earthing arrangements

Final values, protective-device selections, test methods and acceptance criteria must be established from verified site conditions, equipment data and applicable Nigerian requirements and relevant installation standards.
